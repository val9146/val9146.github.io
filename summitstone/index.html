SMS Consent

Tenants may opt in to SummitStone Tenant Notifications by providing their mobile number and checking the SMS consent box in their signed lease, or by giving written consent to SummitStone LLC. By providing your number you agree to receive texts about rent reminders, maintenance scheduling, property notices and replies to your questions. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help. Consent is not a condition of renting. See our SMS Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.
