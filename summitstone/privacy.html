Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how SummitStone LLC ("we", "us"), 30 N Gould St Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801, collects, uses, and protects your information. Contact: val.khazin@summitstone-llc.com.

Information we collect. Name, mobile phone number, email address, mailing address, rental application and lease information, maintenance requests and related photos, and records of your SMS consent and messages exchanged with us.

How we use information. To process rental applications, manage leases, send rent reminders, schedule maintenance, deliver property notices, and respond to your questions.

Sharing. We do not sell personal information. We share information only with vendors and contractors who help us operate our properties or deliver messages, or when required by law.

SMS / Text Messaging

If you opt in to SummitStone Tenant Notifications, we use your mobile number only to send messages about your tenancy and property.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Opt out. Reply STOP to any message at any time to stop receiving texts. You will receive one confirmation message and no further messages.

Help. Reply HELP to any message for help, or email val.khazin@summitstone-llc.com.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. See our SMS Terms and Conditions.

Security and Your Choices

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information. You may contact us at any time to review or update your information.
