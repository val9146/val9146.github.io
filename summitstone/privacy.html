Text Messaging Privacy Policy & Terms

Program description. SummitStone LLC sends and receives text messages (SMS and MMS) with current tenants, prospective tenants, and service providers for tenant and property communication, including maintenance requests, photos of property conditions or repairs, rent and lease reminders, and property notices.

Opt in. You agree to receive text messages from SummitStone LLC when you provide your mobile number to us in your lease or rental application, in writing, or by texting our business number, and confirm that you wish to receive messages. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of renting or of any purchase.

Message frequency. Message frequency varies based on your tenancy and property needs.

Costs. Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Opt out. Reply STOP to any message at any time to stop receiving text messages. You will receive one final message confirming your opt out. To resume, reply START.

Help. Reply HELP to any message for help, or contact us at +1 (332) 296-1231 or val.khazin@summitstone-llc.com.

Privacy of mobile information. We do not share, sell, rent, or trade mobile numbers or SMS opt in data and consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile information is used only to communicate with you about your tenancy and property. Information may be shared only with service providers that help us deliver messages (such as our messaging platform), and as required by law.

Privacy Policy

Information we collect. We collect information you provide to us, such as your name, phone number, email address, mailing address, and information related to your rental application, lease, or maintenance requests.

How we use information. We use your information to process rental applications, manage leases, respond to maintenance requests, send property notices, and communicate with you about your tenancy.

Sharing. We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with vendors and contractors who help us operate and maintain our properties, or when required by law. Mobile numbers and text messaging opt in data are never shared with third parties for marketing.

Security. We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect your information.

Your choices. You may contact us at any time to review or update your information, or to stop receiving text messages by replying STOP.

Contact. Questions about this policy may be sent to val.khazin@summitstone-llc.com or SummitStone LLC, 30 N Gould St, Ste R, Sheridan, WY 82801.
